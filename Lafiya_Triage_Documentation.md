# Lafiya Triage - Non-Technical Overview

## What is Lafiya Triage?
Lafiya Triage is an AI-powered, decision-support web application designed for frontline health workers. The goal of the platform is to help quickly and safely evaluate patients (like children or vulnerable individuals), providing AI-driven guidance on potential illnesses, recommended care steps, and routing to nearby clinics.

## Core Features

### 1. AI Triage Assistant
Health workers can chat or speak naturally with the AI Assistant. 
- **Voice-Enabled**: Users can talk to the app via microphone, and the AI can speak back recommendations using Text-to-Speech, making it easy to use in hands-free environments.
- **Multilingual Support**: Supports localization to help community health workers who speak varying languages or dialects.
- **Symptom Analysis**: The AI takes the symptoms described by the worker and estimates a risk level (Low, Medium, High) along with a likely illness and immediate guidance.

### 2. Clinic Finder
Based on the patient's condition and the worker's approximate location, the app can look up and recommend nearby healthcare facilities for escalation if the patient requires immediate or specialized medical attention.

### 3. Local Health & Climate Updates
The dashboard provides a dedicated section for "Health & Climate Updates". Based on the geographic location, it surfaces relevant news from the Ministry of Health or local meteorological agencies (e.g., upcoming vaccination drives, heatwave warnings, or outbreak alerts) so health workers stay informed of environmental factors.

### 4. Case History & Live Talk Sessions
- **Case History**: Allows workers to see records of past patient interactions and triage decisions for follow-ups.
- **Live Talk Session**: An integrated capability to communicate immediately and log interactive sessions for real-time assessment.

## How It Works (The Non-Technical View)
1. **Intake**: A health worker opens the app and starts a session.
2. **Observation**: They describe the patient's symptoms (e.g., "The child has had a high fever for two days and a rash").
3. **AI Processing**: The system's "brain" (powered by Google's Gemini AI) compares the symptoms against medical knowledge.
4. **Recommendation**: The app displays an easy-to-read, color-coded triage card:
   - *Red/High Risk*: Go to the hospital immediately.
   - *Yellow/Medium Risk*: Observe and visit a clinic soon.
   - *Green/Low Risk*: Home-care instructions provided.
5. **Action**: The app reads out the response and shows a list of nearby clinics.

## Privacy & Security Note
As a decision-support tool, Lafiya Triage is designed to **assist**, not replace, qualified medical professionals. Any data entered regarding patients should adhere to local healthcare data privacy standards.
